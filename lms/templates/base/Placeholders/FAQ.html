Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about CertiChain

General

What is CertiChain?

CertiChain is a comprehensive learning platform designed to provide students, professionals, and organizations with world-class technology education and career development tools.

Is the platform free to use?

Many of our courses and resources are available for free. We also offer premium certifications and enterprise plans for organizations.

Courses & Certifications

How do I earn a digital credential?

You earn a digital credential by successfully completing a course and passing the final assessment. Once earned, you can download your certificate and share your badge on LinkedIn.

Are the courses self-paced?

Yes, most of our courses are self-paced, allowing you to learn at your own convenience and schedule.

Account & Support

How do I reset my password?

You can reset your password by clicking on the "Forgot Password" link on the login page and following the instructions sent to your email.

Can I access courses on mobile?

Yes, our platform is fully responsive and can be accessed on smartphones and tablets.

We're here to help. Reach out to our support team.
